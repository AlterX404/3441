Welcome to The Cambridge School

A place to feel supported, challenged and inspired.

We seek to create an inclusive community in which each individual is cared for and challenged by high expectations to reach their full potential.

Our learning environment combines academic purpose with a rich programme of experiences inside and beyond the classroom. Students are encouraged to take responsibility, use their initiative, communicate confidently and value other people.

We want every student to leave The Cambridge School with a strong sense of purpose, the confidence to make thoughtful choices and the skills to keep learning.
