Join the community

Work with people who care about students and each other.

We look for educators and professionals who combine subject strength with communication, responsibility and a genuine commitment to young people.

Who we look for

People who help students grow.

Vacancies are published on the school website and in newspapers depending on requirements. For teaching roles, candidates should combine strong subject knowledge with communication, classroom presence and confident use of technology.

01

Communication

Fluency and confidence in English, with the ability to explain ideas clearly and engage learners.

02

Subject strength

Sound knowledge of the concerned subject and qualifications appropriate to the position applied for.

03

Digital readiness

Competency in IT skills and a willingness to use digital tools meaningfully in teaching and school work.

04

Student focus

A positive, responsible approach that supports academic progress as well as confidence, values and life skills.

Selection process

Clear, structured and role-focused.

As per applicable CBSE / Government rules and school requirements, screening and selection may include the following stages.

01

Application

Submit your details and latest résumé for consideration against current or upcoming requirements.

02

Screening / Written Test

Shortlisted candidates may be invited for a written or subject-based assessment.

03

Personal Interview

Selected applicants meet the school leadership or recruitment panel for an interview.

04

Lesson Demonstration

Teaching applicants may be asked to conduct a lesson demonstration before final selection.

The exact selection process may vary depending on the position and school requirements.
