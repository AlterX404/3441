Welcome to The Cambridge School

At The Cambridge School, the stated aim is to create an inclusive community in which each individual is cared for and challenged by high expectations to reach their full potential.

The school describes a positive and creative learning environment with a varied programme of experiences and opportunities in school and within the wider community. The intention is to help pupils experience success, develop a positive attitude to learning and life, and build confidence and self-esteem.

Responsibility and self-belief

Students are encouraged to take responsibility, use their own initiative, develop self-confidence, strengthen communication and interaction skills, and value other people. The school also emphasises a well-structured environment for learning and a strong sense of purpose for life beyond school.

Learning built around the individual

The school’s published copy describes an academically rich curriculum intended to meet the unique needs of individual children while respecting their interests and curiosity as learners.
